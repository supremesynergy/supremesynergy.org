A thought experiment from 1902, turned inside out

Poincaré's Ball, Flipped

A finite world that looks infinite from the inside. Step through six beats.

Hot centre (Poincaré)Cold centre (flipped)

Poincaré's original is in Science and Hypothesis (1902), chapter 4. The flip, with zero at the centre, is the same world as ordinary flat space turned inside out around the rim.

Drawn with the ball's radius as 1. Light paths in beats 1 to 3 are traced live from the temperature law. Star distances in beats 4 to 6 are illustrative. The lopsided warp in beat 6 is a drawing of the idea, not a computed model.
